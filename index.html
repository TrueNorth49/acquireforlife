Learn a language by talking.

Acquire drops you into a conversation with a partner who only speaks the language you want to learn. Start at "hi". It gets harder only as your replies prove you can handle it.

No streaks. No word lists you will forget. Just conversations, and words pulled from what you actually said.

How it works

  • Talk. Your partner greets you in the target language and waits. Speak, or type. Turn on meaning subtitles when you need them.
  • Themes. Twenty-four everyday situations, or just talk.
  • Words. Vocabulary from your own conversations, with spoken recall tracked from what you said unaided.

Your learning record stays on your device. Delete the app when you can do it without the app.
