Support

Acquire is in early testing on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight.

For help, bug reports, beta access or any question, email support@acquireforlife.com. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where is my data? Your saved words and conversation history are stored on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account? If you signed in, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Deleting the app removes the on-device learning record.

Why does it need the microphone? Conversations are spoken. Audio is only captured while you are in a conversation and after you have agreed to AI processing.
